Colors

Every finish. One model.

Upload one GLB file. Each WooCommerce variation can recolor it, retexture it or hide parts, and the model updates in place when the shopper picks.

Finish · Sand

Parts

Every part is yours to set.

The editor reads the mesh tree from the file. Show or hide a part, give it a color, a texture, a metal finish, and a label shoppers understand.

Personalization

Their initials. On the model.

Shoppers add text or an image to the areas you allow, then move, resize and rotate it. You set the fonts, colors, character limit and price.

Pricing

The price follows every pick.

Charge for a finish, a part or an engraving, as a fixed amount or a percentage. The cart total is recalculated on your server.

Augmented reality

Customizer

A bigger stage
for the sale.

Customize opens a large dialog with the 3D model, the options, quantity, the live total and your real Add to cart. The model never reloads on the way in or out.

Swatches, thumbnails, buttons

Group options any way you like, set defaults, and make a pick required before Add to cart works.

Rules that keep it sane

Show a group only for certain variations or after an earlier pick. Hidden groups are never charged.

Undo for shoppers

Undo and redo buttons, plus Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z, up to 50 steps back.

Visual editor

Set it all up.
Write no code.

A full-screen 3D editor opens from the product's 3D Model tab. Click a part to select it, change its material, place hotspots, frame the camera and save.
