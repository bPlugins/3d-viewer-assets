Questions

Good to know.

Which 3D files can I use?

GLB and glTF for the model, plus USDZ for AR on iPhone and iPad. Compressed models load too (Draco, Meshopt and KTX2 textures). Models are ordinary Media Library files, and you can also point to a file hosted on a CDN.

Do I need a separate model for every color?

No. Upload one model and set what each WooCommerce variation changes: colors, textures, materials, or which parts show. A variation can also use a different model if it needs one.

Will 3D slow down my product pages?

By default shoppers see a light poster first, and the 3D engine loads only when they click. The engine only loads on pages that need it, and lighting files, decoders and the AR QR code are served from your own store.

Do shoppers need an app for AR?

No. iPhone and iPad use Quick Look with a USDZ file you upload, Android uses Scene Viewer, and desktop shows a QR code. With the “AR of the configured product” module, shoppers see their own colors and parts in AR.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

It stays, unless you turn on “Delete all 3D configurator data” first. Order history is always kept, and turning a module off keeps its settings.

Which products and themes does it work with?

Simple and variable WooCommerce products. It works with classic and block themes, the Cart and Checkout blocks, HPOS, Elementor (with a widget) and variation swatch plugins that use WooCommerce's standard variation events.
