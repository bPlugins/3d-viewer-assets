Colors

Every finish. One model.

Upload one GLB file. Each WooCommerce variation can recolor it, retexture it or hide parts, and the model updates in place when the shopper picks.

Finish · Sand

Parts

Every part is yours to set.

The editor reads the mesh tree from the file. Show or hide a part, give it a color, a texture, a metal finish, and a label shoppers understand.

Personalization

Their initials. On the model.

Shoppers add text or an image to the areas you allow, then move, resize and rotate it. You set the fonts, colors, character limit and price.

Pricing

The price follows every pick.

Charge for a finish, a part or an engraving, as a fixed amount or a percentage. The cart total is recalculated on your server.
